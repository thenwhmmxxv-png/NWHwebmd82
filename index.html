Ventosa / Cupping (8 Methods)
Four core techniques and four specialized methods: Dry, Wet, Running, Flash, Fire, Needle, Herbal and Water cupping.
See the 8 methods
  1. Dry Cupping (Retained) – Cups stay on the skin 5 to 15 minutes. Relieves deep muscle knots and tightness.
  2. Wet Cupping (Hijama) – Suction, tiny sterile skin pricks, then a second suction. Clears localized fluid stagnation.
  3. Running Cupping (Sliding) – Oiled skin lets cups glide across broad muscle groups. Releases wide fascial tension.
  4. Flash Cupping (Empty) – Cups are suctioned on and popped off within seconds. Stimulates blood flow and lymphatic drainage without dark marks.
  5. Fire Cupping (Traditional Ventosa) – A flame burns off the oxygen in the cup before it is pressed on the skin. Strong vacuum with warmth for stiff muscles.
  6. Needle Cupping – Cups are placed over acupuncture needles at meridian or trigger points. Amplifies deep tissue stimulation and blood flow.
  7. Herbal Cupping – Cups are boiled in a herbal decoction such as ginger, mugwort or camphor. Heat and herbal steam with suction.
  8. Water Cupping – Cups are partly filled with warm water, or applied in a warm bath. Gentle, even suction.
